Picturesque Porthleven

Porthleven is a picturesque little coastal town on the south coast of Cornwall. There are plenty of local treasures just waiting to be uncovered. It's many beaches, fabulous seafood restaurants, beautiful harbour and array of independent shops draw people back time and time again. With art galleries and studios to explore; full of works from local painters and craftspeople, it’s all here to take in.

Local Walks & Attractions

Porthleven and its surroundings offer plenty of options for outdoor enthusiasts. There are several beautiful walks to explore around the area, such as the coastal path to Loe Bar and Rinsey Head, both of which boast breathtaking views of the coastline.

Read More

Porthleven's Culinary Delights

Porthleven is well-known for its thriving food and drink scene, with an abundance of local, seasonal produce and fresh seafood. The village has a wide range of restaurants, cafes, and pubs to suit all tastes and budgets. From Michelin-starred restaurants to traditional Cornish pubs, there's something for everyone.
